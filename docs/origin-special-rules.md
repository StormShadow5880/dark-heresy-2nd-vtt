## C. Rules that apply to a roll (bonus, additional degree of success, substitute value)

Not automated, shown as origin bonus text. A tick box "origin bonus applies" in the roll dialogs would cover most of them: Hive World (+20 Navigate (Surface) in closed spaces), Frontier World (+20 Tech-Use for weapon modifications, +10 repairs), Forraliss (+1 degree of success on Charm and Deceive), Dark Echo (+10 per Lore rank on Fear tests), Hive Desoleum (+10 Willpower tests for a sworn oath), The Oath Unspoken (+20 opposed Commerce), Yanth (+20 Medicae/Survival crafting), Port Aquila (Strength or Willpower for Commerce), Hrax (+10 Weapon Skill after the first damage), Heretek (+20 Tech-Use with Forbidden Lore), Imperial Navy (+1 degree of success at short range and in melee), Rogue Trader (+10 Fear tests against aliens, +20 Interaction with aliens), Adeptus Arbites (Willpower Bonus as degrees of success on Intimidate and Interrogation), Adeptus Ministorum (+20 instead of +10 when a Fate Point is spent), Nexum (+1 on the critical effects table).

## D. Fate Point abilities (spend a Fate Point for an effect)

Roles: Assassin (extra damage), Chirurgeon (First Aid success), Hierophant (Charm/Command/Intimidate), Sage (Logic/Lore), Seeker (Awareness/Inquiry), Warrior (substitute the skill bonus for degrees), Ace (Operate/Survival), Crusader (pass a Fear test and extra damage against Fear (X) targets), Fanatic (counts as Hatred). All ✗: there is no central "spend a Fate Point for a bonus" function (Fate is spent by hand with the counter; the fatigue and healing functions have their own buttons).

## E. Combat triggers and conditions

Feral World (Low-Tech weapons lose Primitive and gain Proven (3)), Feudal World (ignores the maximum Agility of armour), Death World and Agri-World (Novo Arcadia) (no +30 for attackers when surprised), Imperial Guard (re-roll damage 1-2 against a target an ally attacked), Desperado (free attack after a move), Penitent (+10 after damage), Kalto, Selvanus Binary (Logic test before using a device), Gregorn, Rund (Hatred from other Ministorum members): ✗ text only.

## F. Warband / GM level or narrative

Quarantine World and Snope's World (Subtlety), Pursuit of Data (Research Station: a related Forbidden Lore at rank 1 when a Scholastic Lore reaches rank 2, the GM decides what is related; this one also fires during XP spending): ✗.

## Summary of what stands out

1. **Needs a decision before it can be automated:** Pellenne (talent or trait), Kul (when the Intelligence Bonus counts), Outcast and Kalto (what exactly counts for fatigue), Adepta Sororitas (Corruption conversion), Daemon World (Psyniscience restriction).
2. **Blocked by missing data:** the mutation table (Table 8-16) and the malignancy table (8-15).
3. **A single general feature would cover most of the rest:** a "this origin or talent applies" tick box with the bonus in the roll dialogs (group C) and a central "spend a Fate Point for ..." action (group D). Both need the exact printed wording per rule to be safe, and the decision how the tick box is shown.


## Adepta Sororitas: Corruption conversion

- Stored background text: "Whenever an Adepta Sororitas Character would gain 1 or more Corruption Points, she gains that many Insanity Points minus 1 (to a minimum of 0) instead." Source: the stored text of the background (expansion content); the publisher's general description may read as a fixed rule rather than a choice. **Discrepancy not resolved.**
- **Project decision:** the player is offered the choice (gain Corruption / gain Insanity minus 1). Examples: 1 → 0, 2 → 1, 4 → 3 (`sororitasInsanity`, tested).
- Only the +/- button on the sheet asks, because that is a rule-based gain. Direct edits of the field, macros and the update hooks are not rewritten; nothing already gained is converted afterwards. The GM gets a third button "GM correction (no rule)" that books the plain value.
- Not covered: Corruption gained in the creation wizard (Mutant background does not apply; Sororitas starts with none) and gains by other automations (none exist).
- Untested in Foundry (dialog).

## Origin bonuses in the skill roll dialog

Code: `rules/origin-bonuses.mjs`, dialog section in `prompts/simple-prompt.mjs`, tests `test/origin-bonuses.test.mjs`. **Untested in Foundry (dialog, chat card).**

- No blanket "+20" box. The dialog lists **named** options of the character's own home world, background and role that fit the skill, each with effect and condition. Nothing is ticked by default; the player judges the condition. The manual modifier and the GM modifiers stay as they were. The chat card shows every applied bonus by name.
- Modifier options (stored wording, group C): Hive World (+20 Navigate (Surface), closed spaces), Frontier World (+20 Tech-Use weapon modifications, +10 repairs), Yanth (+20 Medicae/Survival, Crafting), The Oath Unspoken (+20 Commerce, opposed/Evaluate), Heretek (+20 Tech-Use, unfamiliar device, Forbidden Lore Rank 1+), Rogue Trader Fleet (+20 Interaction with aliens), Penitent (+10 first test after damage; the sheet does not track "first"). Forraliss: +1 degree of success on a successful Charm/Deceive, added after the roll.
- Fate Point options (group D), **one radio choice, never automatic**: standard Fate +10 (from the Ministorum text), Adeptus Ministorum +20 (replaces the +10, not added), automatic success with degrees = characteristic bonus for Hierophant (Charm/Command/Intimidate, Willpower), Sage (Logic/Lore, Intelligence), Seeker (Awareness/Inquiry, Perception), Ace (Operate/Survival, Agility; vehicle/steed condition is the player's), Chirurgeon (Medicae, only after a failed First Aid test: no Fate Point is spent when the test succeeded). Disabled without a Fate Point; the Point is spent once, after the roll, only when the effect took effect.
- Relationship tests resolved by the GM roll elsewhere: the named modifiers are added to the manual modifier, **no Fate Point is spent** (message shown).
- **Not offered (stay as shown origin text):** Assassin, Warrior, Crusader, Fanatic, Ossuar, Tuchulcha, Thaur (attack/damage/Fear effects, no skill roll), Arbites (Willpower Bonus for degrees: wording in the data is garbled), Imperial Navy (Ballistic Skill degree, weapon dialog), Hrax (+10 Weapon Skill after first damage), Hive Desoleum (Willpower oath, no skill), Dark Echo and Rogue Trader Fear (+10 per Lore rank / vs aliens: the Fear test has no dialog), Port Aquila (substitute characteristic), Nexum (critical table).

## Remaining origin rules (second pass)

Tests: `test/private-tables.test.mjs`, `test/fate-spend.test.mjs`, `test/origin-costs.test.mjs`. **All dialogs, chat cards, the sheet buttons and the socket path are untested in Foundry.**

### Private tables 8-15 (Malignancies) and 8-16 (Mutations)
- Two **separate** private data sources (`rules/private-tables.mjs`), handed to the rules as `env.privateTables['8-15' | '8-16']`: a list of `{ name, text?, min?, max? }`. Nothing of them is in the repository, no entry is invented. Without the data every rule that needs a table stays open and visible (wizard panel, creation notes, character flag).
- A rule can **choose** (`mode: 'choose'`: a normal wizard choice from the entries, stored in the draft) or **roll** (`mode: 'roll'`: the stored roll is mapped by `min`/`max`; a result the table does not cover stays open with that message). Resolved results are written to the biography notes with the table.
- The import package that fills `env.privateTables` does not exist yet (the rulebook import produces journal pages, not entries): **source still missing**.

### Exorcised
- The starting Malignancy is **chosen** from Table 8-15 (not rolled, label says so), exactly one; open (`table-open`) as long as the table data is missing.
- Insanity Bonus **+2 for avoiding Fear Tests only** (`fearInsanityBonus`): shown on the Insanity tile tooltip; no +20 on Fear or Willpower tests. There is **no Fear dialog and no stored rule for how the Insanity Bonus avoids a Fear Test**, so the helper is the reusable pre-check input and nothing is decided or rolled automatically. Hook point for later: the place that rolls a Fear test must call `fearInsanityBonus(actor.bio, actor.system.insanityBonus)` before asking for the test.
- "Can never be possessed again by the same Daemon": **documented only**. The system has no Daemon identity on a possession, so there is nothing to compare.

### Shrine World, Faith in the Creed (`rules/fate.mjs`, `Acolyte#spendFate`)
- Every spend of a Fate Point goes through `spendFatePoint`: a Point must be available, then (Shrine World only) 1d10. A 1 = the effect happens and the pool is **not** reduced, 2-10 = spent normally. Result, cost and effect are shown (chat line, roll dialog note, card).
- Used by: skill roll dialog Fate options, re-roll, Eye of Vengeance (the refund returns what was really paid), "Fate removes fatigue", Fate: Heal (the Point is spent before the d10 heal roll now; a failed spend skips the entry).
- **Not** used when the Fate Threshold is burned (different action).
- Exactly once: a repeated request id returns the first result (no second d10, no second cost), a second click while a spend of the same character runs is refused. The healing card keeps its own "working" state on top.

### Highborn, Breeding Counts (`influenceLoss`, `applyInfluenceLoss`, `game.dh.loseInfluence`)
- Rule-based loss N > 0 → `max(1, N − 1)`. Never for gains, 0, initial values, recalculation, GM corrections (`source: 'admin'`) and **voluntary costs** (`source: 'voluntary'`, no advantage is assumed). The loss is booked on the Influence modifier (base and advances stay).
- There still is no automatic Influence-loss event in the system; the operation has to be called by hand or macro: `game.dh.loseInfluence(actor, n, { source: 'rule', reason })`. No existing event was connected because none has clear semantics.

### Garden World, Serenity of the Green (`insanityRemoval`, `origin-actions.mjs`)
- Removing Insanity: 50 XP per point from the character's own pool, **GM approval** (project decision): a leaf button on the Insanity tile asks the points; a player's request goes by socket to the active GM, who confirms; the GM client applies it once (request id seen once, owner check). Booked in the XP log. No Disorder is removed. The general limits for removing Insanity are not stored in the system: **source missing**.
- Shock (Table 8-11) and Mental Trauma (Table 8-13) results: **not in the system data** (only Psychic Phenomena exists), so there is no duration to halve. `halveDurationRoundedUp` is the prepared helper (whole positive durations only, anything else gives no value).

### Outcast and Fatigue
- Only the fatigue threshold counts the Toughness Bonus as 2 higher; no general Toughness or damage reduction change. The FAQ statement that an exhausted characteristic reduces its bonus for the respective use (movement, melee damage, damage reduction) is what the fatigue code already does. **No recursive recalculation of the threshold from the reduced bonus is introduced**; which reading "for determining Fatigue" has beyond the threshold stays an **open interpretation**.

### Origin bonuses still not automated
Hrax, Hive Desoleum, Dark Echo and Rogue Trader Fear bonuses (no Fear dialog), Port Aquila, Nexum, Imperial Navy (weapon dialog), Arbites (garbled stored wording), Assassin/Warrior/Crusader/Fanatic/Ossuar/Tuchulcha/Thaur (attack, damage or Fear effects with no skill roll).
