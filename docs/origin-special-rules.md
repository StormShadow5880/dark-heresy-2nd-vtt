## C. Rules that apply to a roll (bonus, additional degree of success, substitute value)

Not automated, shown as origin bonus text. A tick box "origin bonus applies" in the roll dialogs would cover most of them: Hive World (+20 Navigate (Surface) in closed spaces), Frontier World (+20 Tech-Use for weapon modifications, +10 repairs), Forraliss (+1 degree of success on Charm and Deceive), Dark Echo (+10 per Lore rank on Fear tests), Hive Desoleum (+10 Willpower tests for a sworn oath), The Oath Unspoken (+20 opposed Commerce), Yanth (+20 Medicae/Survival crafting), Port Aquila (Strength or Willpower for Commerce), Hrax (+10 Weapon Skill after the first damage), Heretek (+20 Tech-Use with Forbidden Lore), Imperial Navy (+1 degree of success at short range and in melee), Rogue Trader (+10 Fear tests against aliens, +20 Interaction with aliens), Adeptus Arbites (Willpower Bonus as degrees of success on Intimidate and Interrogation), Adeptus Ministorum (+20 instead of +10 when a Fate Point is spent), Nexum (+1 on the critical effects table).

## D. Fate Point abilities (spend a Fate Point for an effect)

Roles: Assassin (extra damage), Chirurgeon (First Aid success), Hierophant (Charm/Command/Intimidate), Sage (Logic/Lore), Seeker (Awareness/Inquiry), Warrior (substitute the skill bonus for degrees), Ace (Operate/Survival), Crusader (pass a Fear test and extra damage against Fear (X) targets), Fanatic (counts as Hatred). Homeworlds: Ossuar (pass a Fear test), Tuchulcha (Adamantium Faith), Thaur (regain a Fate Point after the first critical damage per session). All ✗: there is no central "spend a Fate Point for a bonus" function (Fate is spent by hand with the counter; the fatigue and healing functions have their own buttons).

## E. Combat triggers and conditions

Feral World (Low-Tech weapons lose Primitive and gain Proven (3)), Feudal World (ignores the maximum Agility of armour), Death World and Agri-World (Novo Arcadia) (no +30 for attackers when surprised), Imperial Guard (re-roll damage 1-2 against a target an ally attacked), Desperado (free attack after a move), Penitent (+10 after damage), Kalto, Selvanus Binary (Logic test before using a device), Gregorn, Rund (Hatred from other Ministorum members): ✗ text only.

## F. Warband / GM level or narrative

Quarantine World and Snope's World (Subtlety), Pursuit of Data (Research Station: a related Forbidden Lore at rank 1 when a Scholastic Lore reaches rank 2, the GM decides what is related; this one also fires during XP spending): ✗.

## Summary of what stands out

1. **Needs a decision before it can be automated:** Pellenne (talent or trait), Kul (when the Intelligence Bonus counts), Outcast and Kalto (what exactly counts for fatigue), Adepta Sororitas (Corruption conversion), Daemon World (Psyniscience restriction).
2. **Blocked by missing data:** the mutation table (Table 8-16) and the malignancy table (8-15).
3. **A single general feature would cover most of the rest:** a "this origin or talent applies" tick box with the bonus in the roll dialogs (group C) and a central "spend a Fate Point for ..." action (group D). Both need the exact printed wording per rule to be safe, and the decision how the tick box is shown.


## Adepta Sororitas: Corruption conversion

- Stored background text: "Whenever an Adepta Sororitas Character would gain 1 or more Corruption Points, she gains that many Insanity Points minus 1 (to a minimum of 0) instead." Source: the stored text of the background (expansion content); the publisher's general description may read as a fixed rule rather than a choice. **Discrepancy not resolved.**
- **Project decision:** the player is offered the choice (gain Corruption / gain Insanity minus 1). Examples: 1 → 0, 2 → 1, 4 → 3 (`sororitasInsanity`, tested).
- Only the +/- button on the sheet asks, because that is a rule-based gain. Direct edits of the field, macros and the update hooks are not rewritten; nothing already gained is converted afterwards. The GM gets a third button "GM correction (no rule)" that books the plain value.
- Not covered: Corruption gained in the creation wizard (Mutant background does not apply; Sororitas starts with none) and gains by other automations (none exist).
- Untested in Foundry (dialog).
